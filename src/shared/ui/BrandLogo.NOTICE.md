# Brand mark sources

These marks identify compatible products and services. Their names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners; inclusion does not imply endorsement.

- Spotify uses the unmodified [Simple Icons Spotify path](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/spotify.svg), retrieved 2026-09-14. The Simple Icons catalog links this entry to Spotify's official [Design and Branding Guidelines](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design) and does not list a separate icon license. Although the Simple Icons repository is distributed under CC0-1.0, its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) expressly says that this does not imply every included brand icon is CC0. Spotify and its marks remain the property of their respective owners. Music Island uses the icon only to identify Spotify in a space-constrained source card; inclusion does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

- OpenAI, Codex, Claude, and the four-tile Microsoft mark use unmodified SVG path data from [`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), retrieved 2026-09-14.
- The Yandex Music 2023 icon is the Yandex-authored geometric mark published by [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yandex_Music_icon_2023.svg), where it is identified as public-domain simple geometry. Trademark restrictions may still apply.
