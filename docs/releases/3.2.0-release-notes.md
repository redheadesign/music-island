## Music Island 3.2.0

**Glass and Classic.** This update opens in Glass. Choose a palette separately from the interface theme in Appearance. You can return to Classic there, and Music Island remembers that choice. The progress bar picks up the artwork colors, and pressing Like animates the heart inside its button.

**Arrange the island.** Drag the progress bar and music buttons between rows, or use the keyboard. Moving everything into one row makes the island shorter. Add the ten-second seek controls or the new speaker button from the catalog. Click the speaker to mute your Windows output, or hover or focus it to adjust the volume.

**Playback background.** The classic wave moves outward and fades when playback pauses. On Windows 11 it also follows the bass while Direct Yandex Music is connected. If your Direct profile previously had audio analysis off, turn on the background in Appearance to try it. The falling particles keep their regular motion with either setting.

**Local dictation.** Supported GigaAM GGUF models can start recognizing speech during pauses while you are still recording. Enable background recognition in Dictation settings to try it. The model catalog now keeps each model’s status, download and recovery actions together.

**Better Voice.** The microphone, processing stage and virtual output are connected across the gaps between their controls, including when the window is narrow.
