## Music Island 3.2.1

- **Calmer playback waves.** Bass gently changes the speed and shape of the rings while their brightness stays steady. With Direct Yandex Music connected, the wave resumes after seeking, switching tracks or pausing and playing again. You can keep the island open throughout.
- **Glass for the island.** Settings, Better Voice and Dictation use a single dark graphite appearance with the familiar cards and spacing. Your saved island layout, material and palette carry over.
- **Simpler Appearance settings.** Choose Classic or Glass just below the draggable controls. The palette, monitor and opening delay are there too. Glass also has a switch for the playback wave.
- **Foxes in both previews.** The fox animation continues when you switch materials. The layout editor now fits narrow windows from its first frame.
