## Music Island 3.2.2

- **Desktop glass on Windows 11.** The island prepares its optical background as you approach the top edge. The GPU lens refracts the desktop image, and system glass takes over if capture cannot start in time.
- **Glass controls and widgets.** The playback bar has its own moving edge reflection. Nearby widgets and their layout previews use the same glass material as the island. The playback rings take their color from the cover, with the theme accent as a fallback.
- **Appearance controls.** Tune transparency, background blur, parallax, highlight brightness and button edge thickness. Advanced settings add per-surface and shader controls with a **Copy JSON** action. Factory presets update once, and customized values carry over.
- **Recording capsule.** Match the island or choose the capsule's material separately. The preview stays pinned while you scroll through its settings.
